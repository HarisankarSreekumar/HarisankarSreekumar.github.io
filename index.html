At the University of Warwick my work sits where data engineering, education policy, and institutional strategy meet. An automated Python pipeline consolidating over 200 institutional reports. A university wide departmental performance review benchmarked against OfS regulatory standards, now the institutional baseline for internal quality reviews. SQL driven extraction from SITS underpinning regulatory, survey, and operational pipelines.
