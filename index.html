Featured case study · Power BI

Contracts Lifecycle & Performance Analysis

A two-page Power BI dashboard for a UK university's contracts process, built from ~6,500 contract records. It measures completion against a 40-working-day target, tracks the open backlog, and shows where in the lifecycle the largest delays sit.

  • ~56% of assessable completed contracts breached the 40-working-day target
  • ~50 working days median completion, against a 40-day target
  • Bottleneck: progressed → pending signature, median ~26 days

MSc dissertation

Retail demand forecasting

An end-to-end XGBoost forecasting system built on 300,000+ retail transactions. Separate models for normal and promotional sales, three-month-ahead multi-output forecasts, and a Promo Sensitivity Index that ranks SKUs by how strongly they respond to campaigns. XGBoost was chosen after comparing LSTM, DeepAR and N-BEATS, and results were presented in a Power BI dashboard.
